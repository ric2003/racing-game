Volcano island

Volcano Island Lowpoly by Animateria, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.

Adapted for the game by removing the ocean plane and scene lights, adjusting materials and placement, hiding the authored clouds, adding a smoke plume and a subtle lava pulse, and blending the island into the horizon. The source file is retained unchanged.
